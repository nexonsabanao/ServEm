# Philippines Launch Gates — Professional Review Checklist

**Not legal, tax, privacy or financial advice.** This document organizes questions for qualified Philippine professionals. Applicability depends on the actual product, contracts, processes, payment mechanics, user types and service categories. Registration or a disclaimer alone does not establish compliance.

## Gate A — Corporate and local operations

- [ ] Decide entity (e.g. OPC/domestic corporation vs sole proprietor only after advice) and confirm SEC/DTI registration route.
- [ ] Confirm BIR registration, invoicing/receipts, books, tax returns and registration details that must be collected/displayed.
- [ ] Confirm applicable LGU business permit, zoning/home-office and other local requirements for the operating address.
- [ ] Assign responsible officers/owners for support, complaints, safety and compliance.
- [ ] Review trademark, domain, software ownership and contractor IP assignment before launch.

## Gate B — Marketplace/e-commerce obligations

- [ ] Philippine counsel maps RA 11967 (Internet Transactions Act), implementing rules and DTI issuances to this app's role as digital platform/e-marketplace.
- [ ] Define provider/merchant verification and any registration evidence to collect, display or retain.
- [ ] Confirm DTI Trustmark status/requirements for the exact business and platform; verify current official guidance directly because guidance and implementation can change.
- [ ] Terms, pricing, fees, transaction details, cancellation/refund and complaint pathways match actual behavior.
- [ ] Build and staff an internal redress mechanism with case ID, response tracking, evidence, decision and escalation. Configure clocks based on legal advice, including the statute's internal redress provisions.
- [ ] Define prohibited-content/service reports, takedown process, appeals and cooperation with lawful requests.

## Gate C — Labor/recruitment and provider classification

- [ ] Counsel reviews actual onboarding, advertising, matching, ranking, acceptance, pricing, assignment, performance control, discipline, customer relationship and payment flows.
- [ ] Determine whether the model or any category may constitute recruitment/placement or require DOLE/private-employment-agency licensing or other authorization.
- [ ] Ensure operational practices match the written customer/provider contracts; contractor labels alone do not decide legal classification.
- [ ] Review professional licensing requirements for every enabled category; don't rely solely on user self-attestation where a credential is required.
- [ ] Document category exclusions and review process for new categories.

## Gate D — Data Privacy Act / NPC

- [ ] Create a field-by-field data inventory (purpose, proposed lawful basis, recipient, storage region, access roles, retention, deletion).
- [ ] Complete a Privacy Impact Assessment before production personal-data processing; document mitigations and residual risks.
- [ ] Assign DPO/privacy responsibility as required/advised; evaluate NPC DPO/system registration triggers against actual data and processing, including automated profiling/decision-making.
- [ ] Privacy notice accurately explains categories, purposes, lawful basis, sharing, cross-border transfer, rights, retention, security contact and complaints.
- [ ] Processor agreements, vendor due diligence, cross-border arrangements, access controls and retention/deletion jobs are in place.
- [ ] Sensitive/ID/payout records are minimized and segregated; legal/tax/dispute retention exceptions are documented.
- [ ] Data-subject access/correction/deletion process is tested; account deletion does not silently erase legally required accounting/dispute records or retain everything forever.
- [ ] Incident plan has clear determination/notification roles and measures applicable notification deadlines from the legally relevant trigger.

## Gate E — Tax and money flow

- [ ] Philippine accountant determines tax registration/reporting, invoicing and withholding obligations for platform, providers and payment structure, including relevant BIR rules for e-marketplaces/digital financial service providers.
- [ ] Calculate sample scenarios for fixed-price and quote services: customer gross, platform commission, PSP fee, tax/withholding, refunds, disputes, chargebacks and provider net payout.
- [ ] Confirm whether platform acts as agent/merchant/collection party in each flow and ensure accounting matches legal contracts and PSP settlement.
- [ ] Do not call the system a wallet or escrow unless the operational and regulatory structure supports that claim.
- [ ] All amounts use safe currency representation and are reconciled to PSP reports. Manual adjustments require approvals and ledger evidence.

## Gate F — BSP/payment provider

- [ ] Ask PSP to confirm in writing it supports the exact marketplace business model, onboarding, provider payouts, refunds, split settlement if any, chargebacks and geography.
- [ ] Counsel checks whether platform activities or payment flow trigger BSP/National Payment Systems Act or other licensing/registration issues.
- [ ] Provider payout eligibility and identity/tax requirements are configured exactly as provider and counsel approve.
- [ ] Sandbox testing covers duplicate/invalid webhooks, status ambiguity, refund, chargeback, settlement delays and reconciliation mismatch.
- [ ] No real money movement until sandbox reconciliation succeeds and written approvals are recorded.

## Gate G — Safety, consumer support and app stores

- [ ] Provider/customer agreements, privacy policy, refund/cancellation policy, community rules, prohibited-service policy and contact information are published and consistent.
- [ ] Reporting/blocking, moderation, takedown, complaint/redress, dispute evidence, escalation and appeal workflows are staffed/tested.
- [ ] Verify Apple App Store and Google Play requirements for UGC, moderation, privacy disclosures, account deletion, age rating, permissions and payment handling against the implemented app and current policies.
- [ ] Insurance broker reviews general liability, cyber/privacy, professional/E&O and other coverage appropriate to enabled categories.
- [ ] Support coverage, safety escalation and incident contacts are truthful and match actual staffing.

## Official sources to re-check at launch

- LawPhil / Official Gazette and SEC, DTI, BIR, DOLE, NPC and BSP official sites.
- Internet Transactions Act (RA 11967): https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html
- Data Privacy Act (RA 10173): https://officialgazette.gov.ph/2012/08/15/republic-act-no-10173/
- National Privacy Commission: https://privacy.gov.ph/
- BSP payments and settlements: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/PaymentsAndSettlements/PaymentsAndSettlements.aspx
- DTI Trustmark portal: https://trustmark.dti.gov.ph/

Store the professional's advice, date, assumptions and scope in a restricted project records system. Re-check when the payment provider, data collected, categories, platform control model or tax flows change.
